# XA-002 X Advisory Investment Policy and Suitability Standards

## Regulatory Basis

X Advisory Partners LLC is a Registered Investment Adviser subject to fiduciary duty under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940. All client communications are additionally governed by SEC Marketing Rule 206(4)-1 and, for materials distributed to retail investors, FINRA Rule 2210 requirements for fair and balanced presentation.

## Suitability Tiers

Client risk profiles are classified into 3 tiers. Conservative tier clients may hold a maximum equity allocation of 40 percent. Balanced tier clients may hold a maximum equity allocation of 70 percent. Growth tier clients may hold a maximum equity allocation of 95 percent.

Any single-stock position for a Conservative tier client is capped at 2 percent of portfolio value. For Balanced tier the cap is 4 percent, and for Growth tier the cap is 5 percent.

## Prohibited Communications

Advisors and any advisory tooling must not present performance projections, price targets, or forward-looking return estimates to clients. Statements of past performance must be accompanied by the standard disclosure that past performance does not guarantee future results.

Automated systems used in advisory workflows are classified as research support tools. They must not generate investment recommendations, suitability determinations, or buy and sell instructions. Any such output must be escalated to a licensed advisor for review.

## Documentation Requirements

Every client interaction that references a specific security must be logged within 24 hours. Logs are retained for a minimum of 5 years in accordance with Advisers Act Rule 204-2 books and records requirements.

Any material cited in a client meeting must be traceable to an identified source document. Uncited assertions are not permitted in client-facing briefings.

## Escalation

Suitability exceptions require written approval from the Chief Compliance Officer. Requests must be submitted at least 3 business days before the intended trade date.
