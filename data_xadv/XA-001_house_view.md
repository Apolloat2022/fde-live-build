# XA-001 X Advisory House View — Q3 2026

## Semiconductors and AI Infrastructure

X Advisory maintains an OVERWEIGHT stance on semiconductor infrastructure with a conviction rating of 4 out of 5. Our thesis is that datacenter capital expenditure remains in a multi-year expansion, but we flag that consensus revenue estimates for 2027 embed assumptions we consider aggressive. Position sizing for this sector is capped at 8 percent of any single client portfolio.

We downgraded conviction from 5 to 4 in June 2026 after channel checks indicated lead times compressing from 52 weeks to 21 weeks, which historically precedes a normalization in pricing power.

## Financials

X Advisory maintains a NEUTRAL stance on large-cap financials with a conviction rating of 3 out of 5. Net interest margin expansion has largely played out. We prefer money-center institutions over regional banks, where we see continued commercial real estate exposure risk averaging 18 percent of loan books.

## Energy

X Advisory maintains an UNDERWEIGHT stance on integrated energy with a conviction rating of 2 out of 5. Position sizing is capped at 4 percent of any single client portfolio.

## Concentration and Risk Limits

No single equity position may exceed 5 percent of a client's total portfolio value at time of purchase. Sector exposure may not exceed 20 percent. Any position that appreciates beyond 7 percent of portfolio value triggers a mandatory rebalancing review within 30 days.

Positions flagged by the concentration monitor must be reviewed by a Senior Portfolio Manager before any additional purchase is executed.

## Review Cadence

The House View is refreshed quarterly. Interim updates are published when a position's conviction rating moves by 2 or more points. Advisors must reference the current quarter's House View in all client-facing materials.
