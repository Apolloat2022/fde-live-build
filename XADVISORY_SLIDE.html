The 15 Minutes Before Every Client Call

X Advisory  |  Pre-Call Brief
Advisors spend 60–90 minutes preparing for each client conversation — pulling quotes, skimming filings, hunting for our own house position. It is slow, it is inconsistent between advisors, and none of it leaves an audit trail. This tool delivers that same brief in under a minute, with a source behind every sentence.

The Problem Today

  • Prep work is repeated by every advisor, for every client, every time
  • Two advisors asked the same question give two different answers
  • Our own research and positions sit in documents nobody has time to open
  • When compliance asks where a number came from, we often cannot say

What It Does

An advisor types a company name or a question. In seconds they get one clear briefing that pulls together:

  • The current market picture
  • What the company told regulators in its own public filings
  • Our firm's position and the limits that apply to that client

Why It Is Trustworthy

  • Every claim shows its source. Click any sentence, see the document it came from
  • It will not guess. No supporting document means it says so instead of inventing an answer
  • Client identities are masked automatically before anything is displayed
  • Every question and answer is logged for review

The Line We Do Not Cross
This tool prepares advisors — it does not advise clients. Ask it whether to buy something, or where a price is heading, and it declines and routes the question to a licensed advisor. That refusal is deliberate: it is what keeps us on the right side of our regulatory obligations, and what lets us deploy this to the whole firm.
The ask: a 6-week pilot with 10 advisors — we measure hours saved, consistency of answers, and compliance review time.
