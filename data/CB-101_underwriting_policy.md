# CB-101 Consumer Loan Underwriting Policy (Rev. 2026-03)

## 1. Scope
This policy governs unsecured consumer installment loans between $5,000 and
$75,000 originated through retail and digital channels.

## 2. Credit Score Thresholds
Applicants with a FICO score of 720 or above are eligible for the Preferred
pricing tier. Applicants scoring between 660 and 719 are eligible for the
Standard tier. Applicants scoring between 600 and 659 may only be approved
with a documented compensating factor and second-level review. Applications
with a FICO score below 600 must be declined at intake and may not be
manually overridden.

## 3. Debt-to-Income Requirements
Maximum back-end debt-to-income ratio is 43 percent for the Standard tier and
50 percent for the Preferred tier. DTI must be computed using verified gross
monthly income, not stated income. Any application exceeding the applicable
DTI ceiling by more than 3 percentage points requires Credit Committee
approval.

## 4. Income Verification
Salaried applicants must provide two most recent pay stubs and one W-2.
Self-employed applicants must provide two years of filed tax returns plus a
year-to-date profit and loss statement. Verbal verification of employment is
valid for 10 business days from the date of the call.

## 5. Compensating Factors
Acceptable compensating factors are: liquid reserves of at least six months of
scheduled payments, continuous employment with the same employer for five or
more years, or a loan-to-value ratio below 60 percent on pledged collateral.
A single compensating factor is sufficient for the 600-659 band; two are
required if DTI also exceeds 43 percent.

## 6. Adverse Action
Any decline decision must generate an adverse action notice within 30 calendar
days under the Equal Credit Opportunity Act, listing the specific principal
reasons for denial.
