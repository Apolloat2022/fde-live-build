# CB-204 Know Your Customer (KYC) and Customer Due Diligence Standard

## 1. Customer Identification Program
At account opening the institution must collect and record: full legal name,
date of birth, residential street address (post office boxes are not
acceptable as a sole address), and a government-issued taxpayer
identification number. Identity must be verified within 30 days of account
opening.

## 2. Risk Rating
Every customer is assigned an inherent risk rating of Low, Medium, or High at
onboarding. The rating is driven by product type, geography, expected
transaction volume, and whether the customer is a politically exposed person.
High-risk ratings require Enhanced Due Diligence before the account is funded.

## 3. Enhanced Due Diligence
Enhanced Due Diligence requires: documented source of wealth, documented
source of funds, senior compliance officer sign-off, and a negative news
media screen refreshed at least annually.

## 4. Periodic Review Cadence
Low-risk customers are reviewed every 36 months. Medium-risk customers are
reviewed every 24 months. High-risk customers are reviewed every 12 months.
A review is also triggered off-cycle by a material change in customer
behavior, such as a transaction pattern inconsistent with the stated purpose
of the account.

## 5. Beneficial Ownership
For legal entity customers, the institution must identify each individual who
directly or indirectly owns 25 percent or more of the equity interests, plus
one individual with significant managerial control.

## 6. Escalation
Suspicious activity identified during any review must be referred to the
Financial Intelligence Unit within 5 business days of detection.
